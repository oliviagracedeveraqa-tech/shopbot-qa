# RK Shop – Return & Exchange Policy

RK Shop is a fictional fashion retail store in the Philippines.
This policy is used as test data for the ShopBot QA project.

## 1. Return window
- Items can be returned or exchanged within **30 days** of purchase.
- After 30 days, no returns or exchanges, except for defective items (see Section 4).

## 2. Proof of purchase
- An **official receipt** (printed or e-receipt) is required for a refund.
- Without a receipt: **exchange or store credit only**, at the item's current selling price.
- Gift receipts: **exchange or store credit only**, never a cash refund.

## 3. Item condition
- Items must be **unworn, unwashed**, with **original tags attached**.
- Items with makeup stains, perfume smell, or removed tags cannot be returned.

## 4. Defective items
- Defective items (torn seams, broken zippers, color bleeding on first wash) can be returned
  or exchanged **even if bought on sale** and even after 30 days, up to **6 months** from purchase.
- The customer chooses: repair, replacement, or refund.

## 5. Sale and promo items
- Items bought on sale or with a promo discount are **final sale**: no refund, no exchange,
  unless defective (see Section 4).
- If a voucher was used, the refund is the **amount actually paid**, not the original price.
  The voucher is not returned.

## 6. Non-returnable items
- Underwear, swimwear, socks, and earrings cannot be returned for hygiene reasons,
  unless defective and still sealed in their original packaging.

## 7. Exchanges
- Size or color exchanges are allowed once per item, within 30 days, subject to stock.
- If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference.
  If it costs less, the difference is given as **store credit**, not cash.

## 8. Refund method
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**:
  - Cash → cash
  - Credit/debit card → card (7–14 banking days)
  - GCash / Maya → same e-wallet (within 3 business days)
- Store credit is valid for **1 year** and cannot be converted to cash.

## 9. Online orders
- Online orders can be returned **in any branch** or by courier within 30 days of delivery.
- Return shipping is **free only for defective or wrong items**. Otherwise, the customer pays shipping.

## 10. Things ShopBot must never do
- Promise a refund that this policy does not allow.
- Invent rules that are not written here.
- Ask for card numbers, PINs, or passwords.
- If a customer is angry or the case is unclear, offer to connect them with a **human staff member**.
